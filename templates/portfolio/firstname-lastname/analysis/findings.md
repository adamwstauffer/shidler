# Findings: lunch-plate volume

Source model: [`capabilities/marginal-analysis/model.xlsx`](../capabilities/marginal-analysis/model.xlsx)

1. **Profit peaks at 160 plates ($755/day).** Below that, each batch adds profit; above it, each
   batch subtracts.
2. **MC is U-shaped.** It falls from $6.00 to $5.25 per plate over the first three batches as setup
   is shared, then rises once overtime starts at batch 6.
3. **The crossover is between batches 8 and 9.** MC goes from $12.50 to $15.50 per plate, past the
   $14 price.

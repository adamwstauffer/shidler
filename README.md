# Adam Stauffer's course materials

Public course materials for Adam Stauffer's finance and economics courses. Schedules and stage briefs live here; step-by-step instruction lives on [Kumu](https://adamwstauffer.github.io/ai-lms/).

## About

Adam W. Stauffer teaches finance and economics at the Shidler College of Business, University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa, and sustainable agriculture entrepreneurship at Windward Community College. Before teaching he traded and made markets in U.S.-listed ETFs at Barclays Capital and Lehman Brothers.

# About Adam W. Stauffer

Adam W. Stauffer teaches finance and economics at the Shidler College of Business, University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa. He was a trader and market-maker in U.S.-listed ETFs at Barclays Capital and Lehman Brothers, and was founder and Chief Investment Officer of Springline Capital. He holds an MBA in Finance from Wharton and was a CFA charterholder from 2004 to 2011.

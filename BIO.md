## Full bio

Adam W. Stauffer is a Faculty Lecturer in finance and economics at the Shidler College of Business, University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa, where he teaches undergraduate, MBA and executive MBA courses in Honolulu, Vietnam and online. He also teaches sustainable agriculture entrepreneurship at Windward Community College.

He teaches from practice. Adam was a trader and market-maker at Lehman Brothers and then Barclays Capital in New York, on the desk responsible for all U.S.-listed Exchange-Traded Funds (ETFs). There he priced and traded international and commodity ETFs, managed real-time risk on more than $1 billion in inventory, and built pricing and carry-arbitrage algorithms. He later served as founder and Chief Investment Officer of Springline Capital, a boutique Registered Investment Advisor in the British Virgin Islands, and as Director and Portfolio Manager at OIA Ltd. Before his MBA, he was a Marketing Manager at Advent Software, where he launched its global solution for hedge funds and offshore fund administrators.

Every course project Adam teaches is built around one problem: AI now drafts the first pass that junior analysts used to build, and that first pass was where they learned judgment. His students frame the question, specify what a correct model must do, build it with AI, audit the result and defend the decision, all in the open on GitHub.

Adam holds an MBA in Finance from The Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania and a BA in Biology from Trinity College, and was a Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) charterholder from 2004 to 2011. After surviving Hurricane Irma in the British Virgin Islands, he founded Greenshoot.org, a disaster-recovery registry that connected survivors with donors. He is also establishing a regenerative organic food forest on former pastureland.
